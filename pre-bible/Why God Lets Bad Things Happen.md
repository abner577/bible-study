2. Emotional way (people who may be going through a tough time)

*Okay now what about all the bad things that happen like: Losing loved ones or sickness?*

Romans 5:2-4 "And we boast in the hope of the glory of god, Not only so, but we also glory in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance, character, and Hope"
- Point is that we cant give a concrete explanation as to why bad things happen to good people and why good people suffer, because at the end of the day we are mere humans we cannot possibly comprehend Gods mind or plan or the reasons why he puts these things in our plan.
- But what we can say is that Suffering --> Produces perserverance --> Produces character

- One reason why god allows us to suffer is beacuse it deepens our character and allows us to be able to relate to others and their hardship. Also inadvertently lets us get closer to God and deepen our relationship with him because we learn to depend and lean on him in hard times.

- Now imagine if everything was perfect: if you always got what you wanted, had all the money, things, etc. --> Do you TRULY think that you could truly appreciate all that you had? Or maybe do you think that God allows bad things to happen to get us to appreicate life in a deeper way? 

- Someone who got laid off probably has a greater appreciate for their job and money, someone who lost their mom has a greater appreciation for their mom, and so on.
- And for someone who lost a loved one (which is such a common example used). In turn they probably have a much bigger appreciation for the loved ones still in their life. 

- Now this isnt to say that suffering is a good thing or we should see it is a good thing because it allows us to develop character and etc. --> But its something you get through, and its something you grow through and come out better on the other side.

#### Specifically for believers: A promise God has made to us:
Romans 8:28 says: "And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose".

- One of the hardest questions that we ask ourselves when something bad happens is: "Why, why did this happen to us" --> But you need to remember one important thing --> You CANT see the full picture of your life. When we suffer oftentimes we focus on where we are and how much we hate it but this verse says: "All things work together for good to those who love God". Meaning that God uses both the good and hard times to shape events for a positive end for his believers.

- When it comes to suffering, you must picture where you are and where God is trying to bring you. Because at the end of the day, if your one of Gods kids, God has a Plan for you life, the bad things that happen, God allows to happen for a reason that you dont know about yet.

*What about for non-believers that dont have the promise that Whatever happens to you, God is working it towards your eternal good?*
- A non-believer still lives under God`s providence. God hasnt stoppsed beign sovereign over their life just because they dont believe in Him. 

1. First we live in a fallen world. Sickness, death, natural disasters, aging, accidents, and suffering are part of a creation that has been damaged by sin.

2. God may use suffering to bring someone toward him.
Imagine someone who has never cared about God. Their life is going well, they feel self-sufficient, and they never seriously think about death, purpose, eternity, or God. Then something happens that completely shakes their worldview.

That suffering might become the very thing that causes them to ask:
- "What actually matters?"
- "What happens after death?"
- "Is there a God?"

- That doesnt mean every suffering person eventually becomes a Christian, but God can use suffering as one of the circumstances through which someone comes to repentance and faith. 

3. God can still being temporary good out of suffering even for someone who never becomes a believer. 
- A tragedy might make someone more compassionate. Losing someone might repair a broken family relationship. Failure might humble someone. 

But there is an important difference between saying:
- “God can bring good out of this.”
&
- “God promises that everything happening to this person will ultimately work for their good.”

- Romans 8:28 gives believers the second promise. Scripture doesn't give that same guarantee to everyone universally.
