**This file really is just for writing things down that I have gotten from certain videos or things that resonated with me**

1. Salvation and Jesus is fully satisfactory and you cant find that anywhere else in the world
- So, something you need to understand about accepting Jesus Christ into your life, accepting Him as your Lord and Savior, loving Him, and following Him, is that it is the reason why so many people want to share the gospel. There are a lot of reasons, but one of the main reasons is how happy people are. There's a reason for this: one of the reasons is that joy is a fruit of the Spirit, meaning when you receive the Holy Spirit, you are naturally more joyful because of the good news.

A big thing is that these people may be much less inclined to indulge in worldly things that you may deem normal because it's so satisfying. Jesus is fully satisfactory. Once we have Jesus in our hearts, we don't need anything else. Sometimes we may slip up, our desires get to us, and we may want other things, but we don't need anything else.

So many believers, most of the time (there are moments of struggle, moments of pain), are fully content with what they have in Jesus, which is why they don't desire to do these things. The world seems normal, like listening to music, having premarital sex, and nothing else is like this. No other worldly thing is like this.

You think about why we sin, why we do these things, like drink, smoke, and having sex before marriage. It's because it's satisfactory to us. It feels good. We get a sense of satisfaction. Maybe we get a sense of pride from doing them, but this is something you need to understand: it's never fully satisfactory. It's never enough. That's why we always keep coming back to it. That's why people get addicted. That's why people keep coming back to the things they do week after week, getting drunk week after week, having sex before marriage week after week.

What's so different about Jesus is that once you receive it, you're satisfied. You don't want, you don't need more. That's how you can truly tell that the things of this world are temporary. They're not satisfactory to you. 

2. Giving advice on how to stop sinning or get out of bad habits
- So it's going to be really hard at first if you try to go cold turkey on a bunch of these so-called bad habits, whether it may be smoking, gambling, drinking, or lusting.

What I would recommend is first seek the love of Jesus. First, fall in love with Jesus and create a relationship with him. Want to be more like him? Want to get to know him more? Therefore, because of that love, you will be more naturally inclined. It will be much, much easier to stop doing these habits, right?

If you try to stop doing these habits out of your own volition because you think it's wrong, or you purely think, "Oh, Jesus doesn't like this. I should stop doing this," or "Hey, if I keep doing this, I'm going to go to hell," it's going to be so, so much harder to stop doing those things. It's just going to be an annoyance in your life, like you're burning yourself.

But if you first fall in love with Jesus and you want to naturally stop doing these things because of your love of Jesus and you want to follow him, then it will become more natural to you. It will become much, much easier. 

But the distinction needs to be made, right? If you truly love Jesus, then you will be more naturally inclined to stop doing these things and want to change. If you say you love Jesus, but you have no desire to change, I'd say you meditate on that. You question: Do you really love him? Do you really want to follow him if you keep doing these things? If you don't want to change, I'm not one to judge. I don't know your heart, but just question yourself and really take a look in the mirror.
