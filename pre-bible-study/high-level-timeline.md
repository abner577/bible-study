## Question:
General history timeline/major events such as the crossing of the red sea, or being stuck out in the desert for x amount of years, general timeline for those things, jesuses rising, the wine thing, fall of mankind, like high-level explanation of the big events

## Answer:

### Old Testament:
1. Creation and the Fall -- Genesis 1-3
- God creates the world and humanity, Adam and Eve disobey God
- Sin, suffering, and death enter the human story
- This is the basic problem that the rest of the Bible ultimately deals with

2. Noah and the Flood
- Humanity becomes deeply corrupt
- God judges the world through the Flood but saves Noah and his family through the ark

3. Abraham and God`s covenant -- around 2000 BC
- God chooses Abraham and promises him descendants, land, and blessing
- Abrahams family eventually becomes the nation of Israel 
- Family line: Abraham --> Issac --> Jacob/Israel --> 12 tribes of Israel

4. Joseph and Israels move to Egypt -- around 1700 BC (Joseph = Son of Jacob)
- Joseph is sold into slavery by his brothers and ends up in Egypt
- He eventually becomes powerful ther and saves his family during a famine. 

5. Moses, the Exodus, and the Red Sea 
- The israelites havebecome slaves in Egypt, God sends Moses to free them, the Ten Plagues occur
- The israelites esacpe Egypt and cross the Red Sea after God parts the waters

6. The Ten Commandments and 40 years in the wilderness
- God gives Israel the Law at Mount Sinai, including the Ten Commandment
- Israel then refuses to trust God and enter the Promised Land 
- Because of this, they wander in the wilderness for 40 years.

7. Joshua and the Promised Land 
- After Moses dies, Joshua leads Israel into the land of Canaan
- This includes the famous story of the walls of Jericho falling.

8. The judges
- Israel repeatedly turns away from God, gets into trouble, cries out, and is rescused

9. King David and Goliath -- around 1000 BC
- David defeats the Philisitne warrior Goliath while still a young man
- David later becomes Israels greatest king, and Jerusalme beomces the center of the kingdom.
- God promises that Davids royal line will have lasting importance.

10. Solomon and the Temple
- David son, Solomon becomes king and builds the Temple in Jerusalme, which becomes the center of Israelite worship

11. Israel divides and the prophets warn the people.
- Israel original was a unified Kingdom: The unified Kingdom of Israel was established in the geographic region previously known as the land of Canaan, which was called the "Promised Land"

- After Solomon, the kingdom splits into Israel in the north and Judah in the south 

12. The Babylonian Exile -- Jerusalem falls in 586 BC
- Babylon conquers Judah and destroys Jerusalem and the Temple
- Many Jews are taken to Babylon (famous Babylonian Exile)

13. The Jews return and rebuild
- Persia later conuqers Bbaylon, jews are allowed to return to Jerusalem, the templre and jerusalem are rebuilt, centuries later, the region eventually comes under Roman control

---

### New Testament
14. Birth of Jesus -- around 6-4 BC
- Jesus is born in Bethlehem and raised in Nazareth
- Christians believe he is the promised Messiah and the Son of God

15. Jesus ministry and miracles
- Jesus teaches about God and the Kingdom of God and performs many miracles such as:
- Turnign water into wine, walking on water, feeding the 5k with 5 loaves and 2 fish, etc

16. Jesus major teachings
- Jesus teaches things such as loving God, loving your neighbor, forgiveness, repentance, and loving your enemies.
- Famous teaching include the Sermon on the Mount, the Good Samaritan, and the Prodigal Son.

17. The Last Supper, Crucifixion, and Resurrection -- around AD 30-33
- Judas Iscariot was the disciple who betrayed Jesus. He handed Jesus over to the religious leaders in the Garden of Gethsemane by identifying him with a kiss.
- Judas betrayed jesus, he is arrested and crucified under Roman authority
- On the third day, Jesus rises from the dead.

18. Pentecost and the beginning of the Church
- After Jesus ascends to heaven, the Holy Spirit comes upon his followers.
- The disciples begin publicly preaching about Jesus, Christianity starts spreading rapidly.

19. Paul and the spread of Christianity
- Paul beings as a persecutor (previously Saul) of Christians.
- After encountering Jesus, he comes one of Christianitys greatest missionaries. 
- He travels throughout the Roman world establishing churches and writing many of the letters found in the New Testament.

20. The future hope
- The Bible ends with Revelation
- The main idea is that evil, sin, suffering, and death will not ultimately win
- Jesus will return, evil will be judged, and creation will ultimately be restored. 

---

## Summary
Creation/Fall → Abraham → Joseph/Egypt → Moses/Exodus → 40 years → Promised Land → David → Solomon/Temple → Exile → Return → Jesus → Miracles/Teaching → Crucifixion → Resurrection → Church/Paul → Christ's return
