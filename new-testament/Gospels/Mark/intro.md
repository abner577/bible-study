## Introduction / Overview of Mark
- Mark is another Gospel account of Jesus life, ministry, death, and resurrection.

Who was Mark?
- John Mark, a Christian associated with the early church. He wasnt one of the 12 apostles, he appears in Acts and was connected with people such as Peter, Paul, and Barnabas.

- Mark is often understood as having written down an account strongly influenced by Peter`s eyewitness preaching and memories of Jesus.

- We arent chronologically continuining after Matthew, we are going to be reading another account of the same general period of history.
