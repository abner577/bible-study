- Like was a physician and companion of Paul. Paul was probably a Gentile and his Gospel emphasizes that Jesus came not only for Israel but for all people. 

- Luke carefully investigated the events surronding Jesus life and arranged them into an orderly account for someone named Theophilus.

Look out for these themes:
1. Jesus as Savior for everyone --> Gentiles, Samaritans, the poor, women, sinners, outsiders, and socially rejected people receive a lot of attention.

2. Lukes gives more detail about Jesus’ birth and childhood, including events surrounding John the Baptist’s birth.

3. There are several famous teachings and parables that only Luke records, such as the Good Samaritan, the Prodigal Son, and the Rich Man and Lazarus.

4. Luke is also part one of a two-volume work: Luke → Acts. Acts continues the story after Jesus’ resurrection and ascension, showing how Christianity spreads through the early church.
