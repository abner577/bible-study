## Acts 15
Chapter Summary: The apostles and church leaders gather in Jerusalem to determine whether Gentile believers must be circumcised and follow the Law of Moses to be saved, ultimately agreeing that salvation comes through the grace of Jesus Christ. Following this decision, Paul and Barnabas disagree over taking John Mark on their next missionary journey, causing them to separate and continue their missions independently.

**The Letter to Gentile Believers**
- Even though the Gentiles didnt need to become Jews in order to be saved, they still had to follow/abstain from 4 things:
 Food sacrificed to idols, Blood, Meat from strangled animals, Sexual immorality

 - These are pratices that were important for maintaining holiness and fellowship between Jewish and Gentile believers. Even though the Old Testament dietary restrictions arent universally binding under the New Covenant, the early Church still gave specific-food related instructions considering their historical setting.

 - Also just a confirmation: Antioch was a major center of Gentile Christianity.

 ## Acts 16
 Chapter Summary: Timothy joins Paul and Silas on their second missionary journey, and a vision leads them to Macedonia, where they begin preaching in Philippi and convert Lydia. After Paul and Silas are imprisoned, God miraculously opens the prison doors through an earthquake, leading to the conversion of their jailer and his household.

1. Timothy joins Paul and Silas 
- This is the same Timothy associated w 2 books of the New Testament. But 1 Timothy and 2 Timothy werent written by Timothy instead were written by Paul to Timothy. Timothy was a young disciple whose mother was Jewish and whose father was Greek. Paul invited him to accompany them on their missionary journey.

2. They eventually reach Philippi
We can identify the connection to the book of Philippians!
- Philippi: The city where Paul establishes a Christian community.
- Philippians: A letter Paul later writes to the Christians living in Philippi.

3. Paul and Silas in Prison
    1. Paul and Silas are imprisoned. Around midnight, they're praying and singing hymns to God while the other prisoners listen.
    2. An earthquake strikes. The prison doors open, and everyone's chains come loose.
    3. The jailer panics. Seeing the doors open, he assumes the prisoners have escaped and prepares to kill himself, likely fearing severe punishment for allowing them to escape.
    4. Paul stops him. He calls out that everyone is still there. The jailer enters, trembling, and asks how he can be saved.
    5. Paul and Silas explain the Gospel. They tell him to believe in Jesus and then speak God's word to everyone in his household.
    6. The jailer takes them into his home. He washes their wounds, he and his household are baptized, and he gives Paul and Silas a meal.
