- ## Acts 15
Chapter Summary: The apostles and church leaders gather in Jerusalem to determine whether Gentile believers must be circumcised and follow the Law of Moses to be saved, ultimately agreeing that salvation comes through the grace of Jesus Christ. **The Letter to Gentile Believers**
- Even though the Gentiles didnt need to become Jews in order to be saved, they still had to follow/abstain from 4 things:
 Food sacrificed to idols, Blood, Meat from strangled animals, Sexual immorality

 - These are pratices that were important for maintaining holiness and fellowship between Jewish and Gentile believers. Even though the Old Testament dietary restrictions arent universally binding under the New Covenant, the early Church still gave specific-food related instructions considering their historical setting.

 - Also just a confirmation: Antioch was a major center of Gentile Christianity.

## Acts 18
Chapter Summary: Paul continues his missionary journey in Corinth, where he meets Aquila and Priscilla, preaches the Gospel, and receives encouragement from the Lord to continue speaking without fear. Later, in Ephesus, Apollos boldly teaches about Jesus despite having incomplete knowledge, and Priscilla and Aquila help him develop a fuller understanding of God's message.

Aquila and Priscilla = Jews that Paul met in Corinth
Apollos = A Jew that came to Ephesus and preached boldly

"Do not be afraid; keep on spreaking, do not be silent" Acts 18:9
- This is a great reminder of the GOD we serve!! We Serve an all-powerful GOD who rules every day. This doesnt mean we wont experience fear, of course our flesh still will but its just such a strong reminder. 

1. Apollos — Speaking Boldly Without Knowing Everything (Verses 24–28)
**"Spoke with great frevor" --> zeōn tō pneumati**
- Zeon: Literally conveys boiling, being hot, or burning with enthusiasm
- Tō pneumati: Means "in spirit," which may describe his inner attitude

- Together these phrases communicates that Apollos was passionate, and deeply committed to proclaiming what he knew about Jesus.

Second:
**"explained to him the way of God more adequately" --> *akribesteron**
- This means more accurately, or with greater exactness. So Apollos wasnt teaching false information. His understanding was accurate as far as it went but it was incomplete. 
- Let this be a reminder that we dont have to know everything before sharing your faith: Apollos didnt possess a complete understanding of everything yet he boldly proclaimed what he knew to be true about Jesus.
- But it should also be a reminder that you have a responsibility to teach accurately and must remain humble enough to be taught.

## Acts 20
Chapter summary: Paul travels through Macedonia and Greece, eventually arriving in Troas, where he brings Eutychus back to life after he falls from a third-story window. Later, Paul meets with the Ephesian elders in Miletus, delivering an emotional farewell in which he warns them of coming dangers, encourages them to protect the church, and expresses his determination to fulfill God's calling despite the suffering ahead.

1. Pauls determination to go to Jerusalem
"However, I consider my life worth nothing to me; my only aim is to finish the race and complete the task the Lord Jesus has given me—the task of testifying to the good news of God's grace." Acts 20:24
- This is such a powerful example of faith, courage, and dedicated. Paul is recognizing that Gods purpose is greater than his desire to protect his own life.

"I am innocent of the blood of any of you" (Acts 20:26–27)
- Paul is essentially saying, that he has faithfully taught them Gods message, he hasnt hidden the truth from them. The key takeaway is that we are responsible for faithfully sharing Gods truth, but we cannot force others to accept it.
