## Acts 1
- After his resurrection, Jesus appeared to his apostles over a period of 40 days, teaching them about the kingdom of God. He instructed them to wait in Jerusalem for the Holy Spirit. Jesus ascended into heaven, two angels told them that Jesus would 1 day return in the same way they had seen him leave. And Matthias is chosen to replace Judas.

## Acts 2
- The day of Pentecost is an event where the Holy Spirit descended upon Jesus apostles and followers in Jerusalem, marking the birth of the Christian Church.
- Fellowship = Shared participation, and communion rooted in a common relationship with God and other believers.

Chapter Summary: The Holy Spirit descends upon the apostles during Pentecost, enabling them to speak in different languages and proclaim the Gospel to people from many nations. Peter addresses the crowd, declaring that Jesus is the promised Messiah, leading thousands to repent, be baptized, and join the growing community of believers.

- When Peter is addressing the crowd he is explaining that the events that they are witnessing fulfill the prophecy of Joel, in which God promised to pour out His Spirit upon His people. Peter is essentially explaining that Jesus death, resurrection, and the arrival of the Holy Spirit are all part of Gods plan.

**Davids Prophecy about Jesus**
- Peter is arguring that David was prophetically speaking about the resurrection of the Messiah, not merely about himself. 

- David died and was buried. His body experienced decay, so Peter argues that David's words ultimately point beyond himself.
- Jesus died but was resurrected. God did not abandon Jesus to death, nor did his body undergo decay.
- Jesus was a descendant of David. God had promised David that one of his descendants would reign on his throne. Peter identifies Jesus as the fulfillment of that promise.

- Then the fellowship of believers was talked about a bit and the significance was that it was not just about believing in Jesus but also about forming a community charaterized by faith, generosity, and care for another.

## Acts 3
Chapter Summary: Peter heals a man who had been unable to walk since birth through the power of Jesus Christ, astonishing the crowd gathered at the temple. Peter uses this miracle to proclaim that Jesus is the promised Messiah, whose suffering and resurrection fulfilled Scripture, and calls the people to repent and turn toward God.

- Peter asks the onlookers why they are surprised and why theyre staring at him as if he healed the man through his own power.

- Peter is saying that Gods promises concerning the Messiah have been fulfilled through Jesus and that people must reprent and turn to God so that their sins may be wiped away.

## Acts 4
Sanhedrin = Supreme Jewish legislative and judicial council in ancient Israel
Anointed = To be smeared or rubbed with sacred oil to set a person or object apart for a specific holy purpose designated by God.

Chapter Summary: Peter and John are brought before the Sanhedrin for preaching about Jesus and healing a lame man, but, empowered by the Holy Spirit, they boldly refuse to stop spreading the Gospel. The believers pray for continued courage and demonstrate their unity and generosity by sharing their possessions and caring for those in need.

1. Notable people mentioned
- Annas: A former high priest who held significant religious influence. Although Caiaphas was the high priest at the time Annas was an important authority.
- Caiaphas: Acting high priest, played a major role in the trial and condemnation of Jesus
- john & Alenxander: Members of high-priestly circle.

2. Peter & John before the Sanhedrin
- Something we can take from Peter and John here is their refusal to listen to the elders orders to not speak Jesus in the town. They said: "Which is right in Gods eyes: to listen to you, or to him? You be the judges!". And thats just so powerful because it reminds us how our priorities in life should be.

3. Believers Pray for Boldness
- Something to note here is that they arent asking God to remove the persecution or take away the challenge. Instead they ask for strength and boldness to fight against it. So its an important reminder that faith doesnt eliminate challenges, it gives us the courage to face those challenges.
