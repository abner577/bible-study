## Section
- Matthew 8-10 froms another major section where Jesus performs miracles to show that he truly has divine authority and that the kingdom of heaven he has been preaching about is actually arriving through him.

- Progression: Jesus teaches what the kingdom of heaven is like → shows through his actions who he is and that he has authority → sends his disciples out to spread that message.

## Matthew 8:
- Centurion: A professional military officer in the Roman army who commanded a unit.

- Jesus demonstrates authority through miracles: over sickness, nature, demons, sin, and even death.
