Explains the genealogy of Jesus the Messiah, we see some names we now recognize here like Abraham --> Issac --> Jacob --> 12 sons/tribes of Israel

- The purpose of this is to prove the identity of Jesus in a way as lineage meant alot back then.

- The main takeaways are that there were 14 generations in all from Abraham to David, 14 from David to the exile to Babylon, and 14 from the exile to the Messiah.

- We also briefly learn about how Jesus is born, how Mary was a virgin but was impregnated by the holy spirit. First Joseph plans to quietly divorce but then an Angel comes and tells him the truth.

- This answers the prophecy of: '“The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel” (which means “God with us”). '
