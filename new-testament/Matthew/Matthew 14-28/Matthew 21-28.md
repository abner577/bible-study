## Matthew 21
- Chapter is about Jesus openly presenting himself as the Messiah and confronting the religious leaders for their hyprocriy, corruption, and rejection of God.

- Jesus enters Jerusalem as king in fulfillment of prophecy, then cleanses the temple beacuse a place meant for worship had been corrupted.

- Jesus challenges the religious leaders, showing them that people who seem outwardly religious can still reject God, while obvious sinners who genuinely repent may enter Gods kingdom ahead of them

- Jesus is emphasizing genuine faith and trust in God, not promising that every personal request will automatically happen exactly as we want. Prayer is still ultimately submitted to God’s will.

## Matthew 22
- THe main theme is that Jesus keeps exposing the religious leaders bad motives and answering their traps with wisdown they cannot refute. 

#### "Whose son is the Messiah sub-section"
- The confusing part is that the Messiah was commonly understood to be the "son of David" meaning a descendant of King David, because it was promised that the Messiah would come through David`s family line.

- Clarification: Mary is Jesus human mother, God is his Father, and his conception is what happened through the Holy Spirit.

## Matthew 23
- Matthew 23 is one long, very direct condemnation of the teachers of the law and Pharisees for hypocrisy. The main issue is that they teach one thing and live another.

- They look righteous and respectable externally but inwardly they are corrupt. The real solution is to clean the inside first, meaning the feart and character --> then the outward life should follow from that.

- He also calls them "blind guides" --> They obsess over minor details while missing what matters more, such as justice, mercy, faithfulness, and genuine devotion to God. Jesus is pointing out that they are focused on the wrong things and have their priorities backward.
