## Matthew 14
- John the Baptists is Beheaded at the request of Herod because his wife basically asked for it.

- When Jesus finds out about what happened he went to a solitary place where large crowds followed him. Here is the famous story where Jesus turns 5 loaves ot bread and 2 fish into food for 5k men.

- Once everyone ate, Jesus dismisses the crowd and walks on water towards his disciples which were on a boat, another miracle.

## Matthew 15
- Defiles = To make something dirty or impured

- Main point made here is that what goes into someones mouth doesnt defile them, but what comes out of their mouth, this is what defiles them.

- Jesus explains that what enters through the mouth goes out of the body on the other way. But what comes out of the mouth is what is coming from the heart.

- And the heart is where these evil things are like sexual immportaly and adultery. 

- An important point is that a Canaanite woman comes up to Jesus and his discples but he initially refuses because he says that we was sent only for the lost sheep of ISrael.

- But after acknowledgeing that she is full of faith he grants her wish and heals her daughter at once. Therefore bringing home the point that God accepts all, we just need to be faithful.

- Then Jesus once again split bread enough for 4k men.
