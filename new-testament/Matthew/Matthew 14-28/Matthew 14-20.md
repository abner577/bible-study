## Matthew 14
- John the Baptists is Beheaded at the request of Herod because his wife basically asked for it.

- When Jesus finds out about what happened he went to a solitary place where large crowds followed him. Here is the famous story where Jesus turns 5 loaves of bread and 2 fish into food for 5k men.

- Once everyone ate, Jesus dismisses the crowd and walks on water towards his disciples which were on a boat, another miracle.

## Matthew 15
- Defiles = To make something dirty or impure

- Main point made here is that what goes into someones mouth doesnt defile them, but what comes out of their mouth, this is what defiles them.

- Jesus explains that what enters through the mouth goes out of the body on the other way. But what comes out of the mouth is what is coming from the heart.

- And the heart is where these evil things are like sexual immportaly and adultery. 

- An important point is that a Canaanite woman comes up to Jesus and his discples but he initially refuses because he says that we was sent only for the lost sheep of Israel.

- But after acknowledgeing that she is full of faith he grants her wish and heals her daughter at once. Therefore bringing home the point that God accepts all, we just need to be faithful.

- Then Jesus once again split bread enough for 4k men.

## Matthew 16
- Jesus alludes to his death and then rising again on the third day. He tells his discples to be careful of the teachings of the Pharisees and the Sadducees. 

- Jesus is declared to be the Messiah by Peter and at the end of the chapter he predicts his death.

## Matthew 17
Transfigured = changed in outward form or appearence, usually into something much more beautiful or spiritual.

- On top of a mountain where Jesus and some of his discples were, it is said that he was transfigured before them as his face shone like the sun and his clothes became as white as the light.

- They then heard a voice from the clouds saying: "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased. Listen to him!"

- Once again Jesus says to not tell anyone of what they have seen here until the Son of Man has been raised from the dead (alluding to his death again).

- He then confirms that John the Baptist was the Elijah that comes first that will prepare the way for the messiah.

- Jesus predicts his death and ressurection a second time as well.

## Matthew 18
- Woe = is a powerful expression of deep grief, sorrow, and a warning of impending divine judgment for sin

- Matthew 18 is largely about how Jesus followers should treat one another, especially concerning humility, sin, conflict, and forgiveness.

- Jesus uses a child to teach that greatness in Gods kingdom comes through humility. 

- Jesus gives a serious warning against causing other belivers to fall into sin. 

- Jesus explains how to deal with another believer who sins against you: first speak privately, then involve a few others if necessary, and finally involve the larger church community if the person refuses to listen. The goal is primarily correction and reconciliation, not punishment or embarrassment.

- Forgiveness isnt something like you should keep count of. Point is that God has forgiven us an enourmous debt of sin, we should also be willing to forgive others. 

## Matthew 19
- eunuch = a physically castrated (without testicles) man.

Continues several of Jesus teachins about marriage, humility, wealth, sacrifice, and what it means to follow God.

- Marriage: God`s original design for marriage is a husband and wife becoming one flexh, so marriage must be a lasting covenant the only exception is when sexual immorality occurs.

- Jesus says its difficult for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven, wealth can create a powerful sense or attachment to this world, making it harder for someone to recognize their dependence on God.
