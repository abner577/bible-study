## Luke 1
- This chapter tells the beginnings of John the Baptist and Jesus side by side. 

- The angel Zechariah first and tells him that Elizabeth a woman that is seemingly unable to have children, that she will have John, Zechariah doubts because they are old and Elizabeth has been unable to have children, so he is temporarily unable to speak. Gabriel later appears separately to Mary and announces Jesus’ birth and she trust in the word wholy.

John's birth announced → miraculous birth to an elderly, previously childless couple.
Jesus' birth announced → an even greater miracle: Mary will conceive while still a virgin through the Holy Spirit.
Mary visits Elizabeth → John, still in Elizabeth's womb, reacts to Jesus' presence, already pointing toward John's future role of preparing the way for Jesus.
Mary's song (the Magnificat) praises God for keeping His promises, showing mercy, lifting up the humble, and fulfilling His covenant promises to Israel.
John is born, Zechariah confirms his name, regains his speech, and then gives his own prophetic song.
Zechariah's song celebrates God's coming salvation and specifically describes John's purpose: he will "go on before the Lord to prepare the way for him."

So the biggest takeaway from Luke 1 is: God's long-promised plan is finally beginning to unfold. John is being prepared as the messenger who will announce and prepare the way, while Jesus is the promised Savior and King toward whom everything is pointing.
