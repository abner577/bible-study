## Luke 17
- At the beginning, Jesus teaches about sin, forgiveness, faith, and humility. The main point is the servant example and being obedient. We arent obedient to put God in our debt or to think that we deserve to be in the kingdom of God or we are owed something. We are worthless servants that are ultimately sinners without Gods grace.

- So we obey God because he is Lord and we are His servants, that is our duty.

#### The coming of the kingdom of God
- It has two parts, at the beginniong the Pharisees ask when the kingdom of God will come and Jesus tells them that "the kingdom of God is in your midst" --> In other words, Gods kingdom has already begun to arrive through Jesus Himself. 

- Then Jesus is describing his second coming as life will see completely normal right up until judgment suddently arrived. Thats why Jesus says His coming will be like "lighting flashing across the sky" it will not be some hidden event that people need to hund down. When the Son of Man is revealed, it will be unmistakable.
