## Mark 9
- The chapter is mainly about the disciples still struggling to understand who Jesus is, what kind of Messiah he is, and what following him actually requires. It includes the Transfiguration, the healing of a boy possessed by a spirit, another prediction of Jesus’ death and resurrection, the disciples arguing about greatness, and Jesus teaching them about humility and sin.

"This kind can come out only by prayer" point when trying to drive out a demon:
- What this is saying is that the disciples had previously been given authority to cast out demons, so they may have started relying on that authority almost automatically. SO this is to remind them that spiritual power isnt something that you control.

- You always remain dependent on God and prayer is representative of God, lean on him in prayer.

- Then the whole talk about bringing in a little child the point is --> A child has very little social status / power so when Jesus says: "Whoever welcomes one of these little children in my name welcomes me," hes teaching that greatness in Gods kingdom isnt about status or recognition its about humbly serving and welcoming people without expecting something back in return.
