## Tips
Here I will put tips that I receive on how to read/analyze the bible so that you may be actually understanding what you read

1. When going into a new book we want to look for 4 things:
- Who is the author of the book, to who is this book to?, why are we writing the book, historical context of the book

2. When you are reading and dont understand what a word means or a couple of verses mean, do not just skip over them, take the time to go back and understand. For we need to remember that quality > quantity. We are not reading the bible to tick off a box or say: "I read x amount of chapter" but to truly dissect and understand the word
